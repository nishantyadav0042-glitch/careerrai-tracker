# CareerRai — Sales & Marketing Playbook
### How to convert your inbound leads into paying students
*Prepared June 2026 · Grounded in the live product, real pricing, and the CAT 2026 calendar*

---

## 0. How to use this document

This is an operating manual, not a brochure. It is built to do one job: **turn the ~5 leads/day you are already paying ~₹500 each to acquire into paid CareerRai members.**

Read it in this order:
1. **§1–3** — what you're selling, to whom, and why it wins. (Get the story straight.)
2. **§4–6** — positioning, competitor context, pricing & offer design. (Decide the deal.)
3. **§7–10** — the actual conversion machine: funnel, WhatsApp + call scripts, objection handling, cadence. (Run it daily.)
4. **§11–12** — numbers to watch and a 7-day sprint to switch this on. (Make it real this week.)

Everything in §1–4 is grounded in the **real product** (features, the daily-log→buddy→session loop, real prices from the codebase, the live demo). Competitor prices in §5 are **directional and must be re-verified** before you quote them to a lead — they change every season.

> ⚠️ **One unit-economics fact to internalize before anything else:** if a lead costs ₹500 and you close **1 in 5**, your cost to acquire a paying student is **₹2,500**. A ₹999 monthly plan that churns in month one *loses you money*. A ₹2,499 or ₹4,499 plan makes you healthy. **This entire playbook is biased toward selling the longer plan**, because that's the difference between a business and a treadmill. (Full math in §11.)

## 1. What CareerRai is — in one breath

> **"Your exam. Our IIM buddy. One honest plan to your dream."**

CareerRai is a **CAT-prep accountability app** where every student gets a dedicated **IIM-alumni mentor ("buddy")** who:
- **tracks their daily study** (the student logs in ~90 seconds a day),
- **debriefs their CAT mock tests** with them (not just "you got 82%ile" — *why*, and what to fix),
- **runs a weekly 1-on-1 Google Meet session**, and
- **is reachable on chat/voice** between sessions.

It is a mobile-first PWA (installs like an app, sends reminders). The core loop is: **Log daily → build a streak → take mocks → your buddy debriefs them with you → your percentile moves.**

**The one-sentence pitch you say out loud:**
> *"Most aspirants take 30 mocks and never learn to analyse even one. CareerRai gives you an IIM senior who tracks you every day and sits with you to fix your mocks — so you actually improve, not just practise."*

## 2. Who we sell to (Ideal Customer Profile)

Your buyer is a **20–27-year-old Indian CAT aspirant**, stressed, competitive, and — critically — **preparing largely alone**. Within that, target these three segments in priority order, because they convert differently:

| # | Segment | Why they buy | The line that lands |
|---|---------|--------------|---------------------|
| **A** | **The lonely self-studier** (working professional or dropper studying without coaching) | No one checks on them; they ghost their own prep | *"Nobody's tracking whether you studied today. That's the problem. Your buddy will."* |
| **B** | **The mock-stuck repeater** (took CAT before, plateaued at 80–90%ile) | They practise endlessly but don't *analyse*; they know coaching didn't fix this | *"You don't need more mocks. You need someone to tell you why your DILR cracks under time."* |
| **C** | **The over-coached but un-mentored** (enrolled in TIME/IMS but lost in a batch of 200) | Paid big money, still feel anonymous and unaccountable | *"Coaching gives you content. We give you a senior who actually knows your name and your weak section."* |

**Emotional reality of all three:** fear of failure, social-comparison anxiety (WhatsApp toppers' groups), burnout, guilt about missed days, family pressure. **Sell to the loneliness and the accountability gap, not to "features."**

**Disqualify fast** (don't waste a ₹500 lead chasing them): aspirants who want a question bank / video lectures / doubt-solving content. CareerRai is **not** a content library (see §4). Saying so honestly *builds* trust and protects your conversion rate.

## 3. Why we win — the wedge

Every competitor sells **content or classes**. You sell **a human who is accountable for one student's progress.** That is the entire moat. Lead with it relentlessly.

**The three differentiators, in priority order:**

1. **A real IIM senior, 1:1 — not a batch.** "IIM graduates. CAT crackers. Your elder sibling in the exam." Every buddy cleared CAT at 95th+ percentile. This is the emotional hook *and* the credibility proof in one.
2. **Daily accountability that actually exists.** The app makes "did you study today?" a real, tracked, nudged question. Streaks, miss-recovery, your buddy seeing your log — this is the behaviour-change engine no PDF can match.
3. **Mock analysis done *with* you.** Your buddy studies your scorecard, names your error patterns (conceptual / silly / time / panic / wrong-selection), and adjusts the plan **week by week, not batch by batch.**

**Positioning statement (memorize this):**
> *"Bharat-first peer mentorship. An IIM buddy who keeps you honest, debriefs your mocks, and shows up every week — for the price of a few coaching classes."*

**Your single most powerful sales asset:** the **live demo** → `https://careerrai-daily.vercel.app/demo`. One tap, no login, drops the lead straight into Aarav's real lived-in dashboard (78-day streak, IIM-Ahmedabad target, a percentile-recovery arc) with a "this is a demo profile" popup. **Send this in the first message to every lead.** Seeing the lived-in product does more than any pitch.

## 4. Be honest about what we are *not* (this protects conversion)

Telling a lead the truth up front kills bad-fit deals early and massively raises trust with good-fit ones. CareerRai today does **not** offer:
- a video/content library or recorded lectures,
- a practice question bank or in-app full mock,
- 24/7 doubt-solving (doubts are handled in the weekly session + chat),
- a peer community / leaderboard (deliberate — no harmful comparison).

**Reframe each "no" as a "yes" to focus:**
> *"We don't dump 500 hours of video on you — you already have YouTube for that. We give you the one thing YouTube can't: a senior who knows your name, your mocks, and whether you actually showed up today."*

## 6. Pricing & offer design

### 6.1 Your real plans (from the product)

| Plan | Price | Per-month effective | Tagline (in-app) | Role in selling |
|------|-------|---------------------|------------------|-----------------|
| **6 Months** ⭐ | **₹4,499** | ~₹750/mo | *"The whole journey to CAT"* | **The hero. Sell this first.** Covers prep straight through CAT (Nov 29). |
| **3 Months** | **₹2,499** | ~₹833/mo | *"One season of prep"* | The fallback / "smaller commitment" close. |
| **1 Month** | **₹999** | ₹999/mo | *"Month to month"* | The *anchor* and the last resort — rarely your goal (see unit economics). |

### 6.2 How to present price (anchoring)

Always show **three options, highest first**, and frame against the CAT date:

> *"There are about 5 months to CAT. The 6-month plan covers your entire run to the exam — that's ₹4,499, basically ₹750 a month for a personal IIM mentor. Most people take that. If you want to start smaller, 3 months is ₹2,499. There's a monthly at ₹999 too."*

The ₹999 monthly exists to make ₹4,499 *feel small per month* — not to be sold. When a lead anchors on "₹999," walk them up: *"That's fine — though at ₹999×5 that's ₹4,995 anyway, and the 6-month plan is ₹4,499 with no re-deciding every month. One decision, done."*

### 6.3 Founder-cohort framing (use the truth you already have)

You are a **founding cohort** with **"200+ IIM mentors ready."** That is genuine scarcity + status. Use it:
> *"You'd be in our founding cohort — early students get the most mentor attention and a locked founder price. We're keeping this batch small on purpose."*

### 6.4 Levers you already have built in (use deliberately)

The product already supports **scholarships** and **coupons** server-side. Deploy them as *closing tools*, never as a default discount:
- **Scholarship** → for genuine hardship / high-potential droppers. Frame as *earned*: *"I can put you up for a founder scholarship — tell me your situation."* (Earned discounts don't cheapen the brand; blanket discounts do.)
- **Coupon (e.g. `FOUNDER20`)** → a *time-boxed* close: *"If you start today I'll apply the founding-cohort code — it's only on for this week's intake."*

### 6.5 De-risk the purchase

The #1 objection is trust ("will a stranger actually show up?"). Neutralize it with a **structural guarantee**, e.g.:
> *"Do your first week. If your buddy doesn't show up and debrief your first mock, full refund — no questions."*

A first-week / first-session guarantee costs you almost nothing (your buddies *do* show up) and removes the single biggest reason a lead stalls. The product already has a refund path built in.

## 7. The conversion machine — funnel for 5 leads/day

You get ~5 leads/day (~150/month). Treat each like the ₹500 it cost. The funnel has five stages; **the demo and the call are where deals are won.**

```
   LEAD              DEMO              CALL              CLOSE            ONBOARD
 (₹500 each)  →   (send link)   →  (15-min call)  →   (pay link)   →   (Day-1 log)
   ~150/mo         target 70%        target 50%         target 40%        target 95%
                   open the demo     take the call      of called pay     activated
```

**Realistic target math (tune with your real data):**
- 150 leads → ~105 open the demo → ~52 take a call → **~21 paying students/month.**
- At a blended ~₹3,000 first purchase → **~₹63,000/month** from leads that cost you ~₹75,000. **This is exactly why you must (a) push 3/6-month plans and (b) drive the close-rate up — the playbook below is how.**

**The golden rule of speed:** an Indian edtech lead goes cold in *hours*. **Contact every new lead within 5 minutes on WhatsApp.** Speed-to-lead is the single highest-leverage variable you control.

## 8. Channel playbook — WhatsApp + call scripts

WhatsApp is your primary channel; a short call is your closer. Scripts below are **templates — personalize the first line every time** (use the lead's name and, if you have it from the CAT-readiness diagnostic, their weak section).

### 8.1 WhatsApp sequence (the core engine)

**MSG 1 — within 5 minutes of the lead (the demo drop):**
> Hi {Name}! 👋 This is {You} from CareerRai. You checked out our CAT prep + IIM buddy program.
> Before I explain anything — just *see* it. This is a real student's live dashboard (no login needed):
> 👉 https://careerrai-daily.vercel.app/demo
> That's what your daily prep + IIM mentor looks like. Took a look? 🙂

**MSG 2 — same day if no reply (value + soft CTA):**
> Quick why-this-matters: most aspirants take 30+ mocks and never learn to analyse one. Your IIM buddy sits with you on a weekly call and fixes exactly that — plus keeps you honest daily.
> CAT 2026 is **29 Nov** — about 5 months. Want a free 15-min call with an IIM buddy to map your plan? I have slots today/tomorrow.

**MSG 3 — Day 2 (proof + scarcity):**
> {Name}, every CareerRai buddy is an IIM grad who cracked CAT at 95th+ %ile — your "elder sibling" in the exam, not a teacher in a batch of 200.
> We're keeping the founding cohort small. Want me to hold a founder spot for you? 🎯

**MSG 4 — Day 4 (the honest nudge):**
> No pressure at all 🙏 — just don't want you grinding alone for 5 months. If now's not right, tell me and I'll check back closer to the exam. If you're in, I'll set up your buddy call today.

**MSG 5 — Day 7 (the close or park):**
> Last note from me for now 🙂 Founding-cohort price + a free first-week (full refund if your buddy doesn't show up and debrief your first mock). Want me to send the link?

> **Cadence rule:** 5 touches over 7 days, then move to a monthly "closer to CAT" re-engagement list. Stop when they say stop. Never spam — one thoughtful message beats five nags.

### 8.2 The 15-minute discovery + close call

**Structure (don't lecture — diagnose):**
1. **(2 min) Rapport + their why:** *"What's your CAT target, and what's been the hardest part of prep so far?"* → **Listen for the pain** (alone / mocks not improving / inconsistent). This is your ammo.
2. **(3 min) Mirror the pain:** *"So you're studying but no one's checking if you actually do it, and your mock scores aren't moving — right?"* (Get a "yes.")
3. **(4 min) Show, don't tell:** screen-share the demo / their CAT-readiness result. *"Here's exactly how your buddy would track this and debrief your next mock."*
4. **(2 min) The buddy match:** *"I'd match you with a buddy whose CAT profile fits your weak section — intentionally, not randomly."*
5. **(3 min) Price + close:** anchor 6-month first (§6.2), then: *"Should I set you up with the 6-month or start you on 3? I can send the link right now and your buddy onboards you today."* → **Assume the sale. Send the payment link on the call.**

**Golden close line:**
> *"You're going to spend the next 5 months preparing anyway. The only question is whether you do it alone or with an IIM senior tracking you. Let's not do it alone."*

## 10. Follow-up cadence & lead hygiene

- **New lead:** WhatsApp in **≤5 min**. Demo link in message 1, always.
- **Engaged but unclosed:** 5 touches / 7 days (§8.1), then **monthly re-engagement** until CAT.
- **Said "later":** tag with a reason + a date; CAT season (Aug–Nov) panic will reopen many of them. A *"3 months to CAT — still going it alone?"* message in late August converts droppers who said no in June.
- **Closed-won:** hand to buddy within 24h; **the Day-1 log is the activation moment** — make sure their buddy messages them the same day. Activation = retention = LTV.
- **Track every lead** in one sheet/CRM: Name · Source · Date · Stage · Last touch · Next action · Reason-if-lost. You're paying ₹500 each — none should fall through a crack.

## 11. The numbers that matter (unit economics)

**Inputs (yours, confirm):** ~₹500 / lead · ~5 leads/day · plans ₹999 / ₹2,499 / ₹4,499.

**CAC at different close rates (CAC = ₹500 ÷ close-rate):**

| Close rate | Leads per sale | CAC | Verdict vs. plan |
|------------|----------------|-----|------------------|
| 1 in 10 (10%) | 10 | ₹5,000 | ❌ Underwater on every plan. Fix the funnel. |
| 1 in 5 (20%) | 5 | **₹2,500** | ⚠️ Loses on monthly (₹999); **wins on 3mo/6mo.** |
| 1 in 4 (25%) | 4 | ₹2,000 | ✅ Healthy on 3mo/6mo. |
| 1 in 3 (33%) | 3 | ₹1,500 | ✅✅ Strong. |

**Three conclusions that should drive every decision:**
1. **Sell the 3- and 6-month plans.** At a ₹2,500 CAC, a ₹999 monthly that churns is a *loss*; a ₹4,499 plan is a clean ~₹2,000 contribution before delivery cost. The plan you sell matters more than almost anything else.
2. **Close rate is everything.** Going 20% → 25% nearly doubles your margin. Speed-to-lead (§7) and the demo (§3) are your two biggest close-rate levers.
3. **Watch delivery margin.** Your real cost is **buddy time** (weekly 1:1s). Track buddy : student ratios so the mentorship promise stays profitable as you scale — this is the model's true constraint.

**North-star weekly dashboard:** Leads · Demo-open % · Call-booked % · **Close %** · **Avg plan value** · CAC · Activation (Day-1 log) % · Week-2 retention.
