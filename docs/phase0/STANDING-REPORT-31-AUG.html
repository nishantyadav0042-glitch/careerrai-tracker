Students can now see the links

The verified resource map was being attached to tasks by the engine and read by nobody. It renders now — one optional link under the task, opening the original watch page in the student's own browser, with the channel and real runtime shown before the tap.

All three Phase-0 topics — Reading Comprehension, Percentages, Arrangements — resolve at every phase, so a day-one student sees this.

Four rules are enforced by a guard test rather than trusted to reviewers, because each is the kind of rule that erodes one reasonable edit at a time:

Never hosted

guarded

An anchor with target=_blank. No iframe, no /embed, no proxy. An embed would put someone else's video inside our chrome and make us look like the publisher.

Never mandatory

guarded

The row says "optional", touches nothing in the completion path, and stops its own taps from reaching the task row. Incident #2 is the standing lesson — a required extra tick cost a whole cohort.

One link, never a list

guarded

The guard counts anchors. A list hands the decision back to a student who came here to be told what to do next.

The source is always named

guarded

Channel and platform-read runtime, before the tap. Twenty-two researched durations were wrong, one by more than twenty minutes.

Three events measure it: resource_shown separates "ignored the link" from "never saw the link", resource_opened records the departure, and resource_verdict is one tap on return. That last one is the entire outcome signal — on YouTube we can see nothing, so it is self-reported by whoever chooses to answer.

The overnight finding

The ladder level is the least trustworthy thing we have

Two unrelated methods reached this independently in the same night, which is why it now outranks the runtime check in the queue.

From searching titles: real video IDs keep coming back under invented titles — a descriptive subtitle after a colon that the real title does not contain. The L1–L4 level was assigned from those subtitles. If the subtitle was generated, so was the level.

From re-reading your own pasted messages: of 33 videos that appear in more than one response, the question count disagrees with itself in 8, the duration in 8, and the assigned level in 5. The same video is graded L1 in one run and L2 in another.

A resource at the wrong rung breaks the task–resource contract as surely as a dead link does. The task says fifteen easy questions; the video delivers a CAT-hard marathon.

A concrete case found this morning: JU-b_Zu-z7U was filed as Divisibility L3 under the title "Numbers 3: Prime Composite Numbers Divisibility". The real video is "Numbers 3 || Number Systems" — a general number-systems lecture, not a divisibility drill. Real video, wrong shelf.

A second pattern worth your eye: several confirmed-real videos are 2019 or 2024 uploads presented under a "for CAT 2026" title the real video never carried. Rodha genuinely runs both series, and the research mixed the two naming conventions.

Two of my own findings I had to withdraw

"Eight round-2 IDs are fabricated"

withdrawn

Based on empty bare-ID searches. Calibrating against four IDs vidIQ had already confirmed real, all four returned nothing — search engines do not index bare video IDs. The finding was an artifact of my method. jdusH0OI-jY, one of those eight, has since been positively confirmed real, which is the proof rather than the argument.

"Fifteen videos carry a heavy paid push"

withdrawn

My search for the word matched the unfilled template string (none / mild / heavy). The real count is three, none shipped.

Blocked

vidIQ cannot reach this session

vidIQ, Exa and Firecrawl are authenticated at your account but report enabledInChat: false here, and their tools are absent from this session's registry. Direct network routes are closed too — YouTube oEmbed and every Invidious/Piped mirror are blocked by the environment's egress policy.

Toggling the connector on mid-session does not inject its tools into a session already running; the tool list is loaded once, at session start. The 71 runtimes need a fresh session with those three toggled on before it opens. It is one batch call once it can run.
