==============================================================
WHAT THIS DOES
==============================================================

Every day at 2 AM UTC:
  ✅ Finds audio files older than 10 days
  ✅ Deletes them from storage (voice-notes bucket)
  ✅ Removes records from database
  ✅ Frees up storage space
  ✅ Logs results for monitoring

Example:
  Before: 15 GB of audio files
  After:  5 GB (old files deleted)
  Saved:  10 GB = $0.60/month savings
